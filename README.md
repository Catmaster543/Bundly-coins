# Pouchy coins
**A simple and lightweight Minecraft Mod that focuses on adding a simple and convenient currency for servers/modpacks, whilst keeping the vanilla feel**. The mod **adds coins of 5 tiers** (following the progression of vanilla => Copper, Iron, Gold, etc.) and **19 unique pouches** to store the coins. You can download the mod from either: <br/>
#### [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/pouchy-coins/) <br/>
#### Modrinth (Currently under review, once approved I'll update this.) <br/>

# Features
### 5 tiers of Coins
**Copper, Iron, Gold, Diamond & Netherite**. Each of these coins have different values, with **iron coins** acting as a sort of base of **1.0 C**. Stack of Iron coins then hold the same value as one gold coin, aka. 64.0 C. <br/>

### 19 Unique pouches
Pouches also kinda follow the **tiers** of coins: **Basic, Copper, Iron, Gold, Diamond & Netherite**. But compared to coins, pouches have different "sizes" on each tier. For example, the basic tier has 4 sizes: small, normal, big and large. <br/>

### Pouch storage
Apart from being cosmetic, **pouches have** a certain amount of **slots** dedicated **for coins**, that varies by the tier and size. <br/> `Note: Only coins can be placed inside the slots of any pouch!` <br/>
You can access any pouches storage by right-clicking it with your hand, which will show the contents of that pouch. And here the pouch works just like any other storage GUI in Minecraft. <br/>

### Coins and Pouches value tooltips
All coins have a tooltip, showing the value of their current stack. So a stack of 42 Iron coins will show you "42.0 C". Add one more iron coin and the value flips to 43. <br/>
Pouches also have their tooltip that works just about as you'd expect: they display the total value of all coins stored inside.

### Crafting recipes for pouches
Pouches can be upgraded by combining it with other pouches and extra ingredients, with each new pouch having it's own unique recipe. <br/>
